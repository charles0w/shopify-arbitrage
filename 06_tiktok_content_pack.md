# TikTok content pack

At your $20 budget, **TikTok organic is your single biggest unlock.** It's the only channel that can give a brand-new arbitrage store a five-figure month with zero ad spend. Every successful small Shopify store you've seen "appear out of nowhere" was running this playbook.

This doc gives you: the strategy, posting cadence, 30 ready-to-film hooks, and a hashtag/audio approach.

---

## The strategy in 60 seconds

- **Make a TikTok account dedicated to the store.** Username = brand name. Bio = one line + Shopify link.
- **Post 3–5 short videos per day for 30 days.** Yes, every day. Yes, that many.
- **Each video is 7–25 seconds.** Phone camera, natural light. Show the product in use, not in a box.
- **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds.** First frame must stop the scroll.
- **Don't try to be polished.** "Made on a phone" outperforms "made by a marketing team" 9 times out of 10 in this category.
- **The algorithm rewards consistency over quality.** One viral post in 30 days pays for everything.

This works because TikTok's For You page treats every video as a fresh test. You're not building a follower base — you're rolling dice on hooks. More dice = more chances at the one that hits.

## Account setup

**Username:** `@[brand]` — same as your store name
**Display name:** `[Brand] · curated finds` (or similar)
**Bio:**
```
✨ everyday goods, hand-picked
🚚 free US shipping over $50
🔗 shop ↓
```
**Link:** Use the free **Linktree** or just link directly to your store homepage. Once you hit 1000 followers, TikTok lets you put the link directly in your bio with a CTA.

**Profile pic:** Logo on a clean color background. Use Canva — takes 5 minutes.

## Posting cadence (first 30 days)

| Days | Posts/day | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1–7 | 3 | Test 3 different hooks per product daily |
| 8–14 | 4 | Add 1 trend-jacked video (use trending audio) |
| 15–30 | 5 | Double down on whatever format got the most views |

**Best posting times** (Pacific): 6–9am, 12–1pm, 7–11pm. Use TikTok's analytics tab (unlocks at 100 followers) to see when YOUR audience is online.

## 30 ready-to-film video hooks

Pick a product, swap `[PRODUCT]` for it, and film. Each takes ~5 minutes to shoot.

### Curiosity / discovery hooks

1. "Things I didn't know I needed until I bought them" — show 3 products fast
2. "POV: you just discovered a small Shopify store with the BEST finds" — slow zoom on product
3. "5 products under $30 that feel like cheat codes for [daily life / your apartment / college / the gym]"
4. "Things from my online store I'm obsessed with — part 1"
5. "Tell me why nobody told me about this" — show product surprise reveal
6. "This is the kind of thing I would've impulse-bought at 1am — except I sell it now"
7. "Random products that fix tiny annoyances you've just lived with"
8. "Stuff that's gone weirdly viral in my Shopify store"

### Demonstration hooks

9. "Watch what this does in 10 seconds" — show product in use, fast
10. "Things that are way better than they need to be" — dramatic close-up reveal
11. "I tested 3 [category] products. This is the only one I kept." — comparison
12. "If you have [common problem], stop scrolling" — direct demonstration
13. "This took me 2 minutes. Worth it." — before/after

### Story / personality hooks

14. "I quit [whatever] to run a small Shopify store. Here's what I'm selling this week."
15. "Day in the life packing orders for my online store"
16. "Show me your [category] without showing me your [category]" (trending format)
17. "Why I started a Shopify store as a Berkeley student" — face to camera, 20 seconds
18. "Replying to @[someone]: 'where do you find these?'"
19. "Storytime: how this product ended up in my store"
20. "Things I would buy if I was [target customer profile]"

### Proof / social hooks

21. "First sale!! 🎉" — film yourself getting the order ping (massive engagement, ONLY use after first sale)
22. "Customer review of the week" — show screenshot + product
23. "When the order count hits double digits" — relatable founder content
24. "Packing the [Nth] order today" — show packaging process
25. "What's actually selling on my Shopify store this week"

### Trend-jack templates (replace audio with current trending sound)

26. "[Trending sound] but I'm using it to show off this [product]"
27. "Tell me you sell stuff online without telling me you sell stuff online"
28. "Things that just make sense" — fast cuts of products solving problems
29. "POV: it's Sunday and the [product] just arrived"
30. "Rate this find: 1–10"

## Hashtag strategy

Use **3–5 hashtags max.** More = looks spammy, performs worse than 4.

**Mix per video:**
- 1 broad: `#tiktokmademebuyit` `#fyp` (only if relevant — don't spam fyp)
- 1 medium: `#smallbusiness` `#smallshopify` `#amazonfinds`
- 1 niche: relevant to product (`#deskaccessories` `#kitchengadgets`)
- 1 community: `#smallbusinesscheck` `#shopifystore`

**Rotate hashtags** between videos — the algorithm sees identical hashtags across all your posts as a low-quality signal.

---

## Audio strategy

**Always use trending sounds.** TikTok's algorithm boosts videos using audio that's currently spreading. Two ways to find them:

1. **In TikTok app:** Tap "+" to create → tap "Add sound" → "Discover" tab → see what's trending. The little upward-arrow icon = trending.
2. **Save audios you scroll past on FYP** that are trending. Hit "Add to favorites" on any sound and it's in your library forever.

Even for product demo videos where you're talking, layer a low-volume trending sound underneath — TikTok still gives you the algorithmic boost.

## What "viral" actually looks like

For a small store starting from zero:
- 80% of your videos will get **<200 views** — that's normal, ignore them
- 15% will get **500–5,000** — these are signal you can use
- 5% will get **10k+** — these are the ones that drive sales
- ~1 in 100 videos will hit **100k+** — these are the ones that pay for everything

**The goal isn't viral. The goal is volume.** 30 days × 4 posts = 120 chances.

---

## When to stop being free and add paid

Once you've gotten your **first 5–10 organic sales** and you have at least one TikTok video with 10k+ views:

1. Take that video. Don't make a "proper ad."
2. Boost it via TikTok Spark Ads (uses your existing organic post as the ad creative — much cheaper CPMs than fresh ad creative)
3. Start at $20/day for 3 days. THIS is when paid traffic makes sense — you're amplifying something proven.

That's the moment your store goes from "fun side project" to "compounding business."
