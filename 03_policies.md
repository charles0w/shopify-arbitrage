# Policy pages

> **⚠️ Read first:** I'm not a lawyer. These are starting templates that cover the basics most small Shopify stores need. Before publishing, do **two** things:
>
> 1. Use Shopify's **free policy generator** (Settings → Policies → "Generate from template") for Privacy and Terms — those have legal nuances that are jurisdiction-dependent and Shopify's generator is reasonable.
> 2. Skim each one yourself and adjust anything that doesn't match how you actually operate (shipping times, return windows, etc.)
>
> Why this matters beyond legal: **Meta and TikTok will reject your ad accounts** if your store is missing visible policy pages. Fix this before you spend a dollar on ads.

---

## 1. Shipping Policy

```
Shipping Policy

Where we ship
We currently ship to all 50 U.S. states. International shipping is
not yet available — sign up for our newsletter to be notified when
we expand.

Processing time
Orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Mon–Fri, excluding
U.S. holidays). You'll receive an email with tracking info as soon
as your order ships.

Delivery time
Standard shipping: 5–10 business days after processing.
Express shipping (where available): 2–4 business days.

Shipping costs
- Standard shipping: $4.95 flat rate
- Free standard shipping on orders over $50
- Express options shown at checkout

Tracking
Every order ships with tracking. If you haven't received tracking
within 3 business days of placing your order, email us at
hello@[BRAND].com and we'll look into it immediately.

Lost or stuck packages
Once a package leaves us, it's in the carrier's hands — but we'll
help. If your tracking hasn't updated for 7+ days or your package
is marked delivered but missing, email us within 30 days of the
order date and we'll work with the carrier or send a replacement.

Address errors
We can't be responsible for orders shipped to incorrect addresses
provided at checkout. Please double-check your shipping address
before completing your order. If you spot a mistake right after
ordering, email us within 1 hour and we'll do our best to update
it before the order ships.
```

> **Action items:** Adjust the shipping times to match your actual supplier's timelines. If you're using AliExpress/CJ Dropshipping with 2–3 week delivery, **be honest about it** — say "10–20 business days." Customers tolerate slow shipping if they're warned. They will chargeback if they're surprised.

## 2. Returns & Refunds Policy

```
Returns & Refunds

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right,
here's how we make it right.

30-day return window
You may return any unused, unopened item within 30 days of delivery
for a full refund of the product price (original shipping is non-
refundable).

How to start a return
Email hello@[BRAND].com with:
  • Your order number
  • The item(s) you'd like to return
  • A short note on why (helps us improve)

We'll respond within 24 hours with a return address and instructions.

Return shipping
Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item
arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect — in those cases, we cover
return shipping and send a replacement at no charge.

Refunds
Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we'll process your
refund to the original payment method within 3–5 business days.
You'll receive an email confirmation when the refund goes through.

Damaged or wrong items
If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item,
email us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We'll send a
replacement immediately or issue a full refund — your choice.

Non-returnable items
For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items can't be
returned once opened:
  • Personal care items
  • Intimate apparel
  • Consumables / food items
  • Final sale items (clearly marked at checkout)

Questions? Email hello@[BRAND].com — a real human will reply.
```

## 3. Privacy Policy

> **Use Shopify's generator for this one.** Settings → Policies → click "Replace with template" under Privacy Policy. Shopify generates a GDPR/CCPA-aware version pre-filled with your store info. Do NOT just paste a random template here — privacy policies have actual legal exposure.

Shopify's generator handles:
- What data you collect (Shopify knows: name, email, shipping address, payment info, etc.)
- Cookies / analytics
- Third-party processors (Stripe, Meta Pixel, Klaviyo, etc.)
- GDPR, CCPA rights
- Contact info for data requests

After generating: scan it once, fill in any `[STORE NAME]` blanks, publish.

---

## 4. Terms of Service

> **Same as Privacy** — use Shopify's generator. Settings → Policies → "Replace with template" under Terms of Service.

Generated terms cover purchases, accuracy of info, prohibited uses, governing law, etc. Solid baseline for a store your size.

## Publishing checklist

In Shopify Admin:

- [ ] Settings → Policies → Generate Privacy Policy from template
- [ ] Settings → Policies → Generate Terms of Service from template
- [ ] Settings → Policies → Paste Refund Policy (above) into "Refund policy"
- [ ] Settings → Policies → Paste Shipping Policy (above) into "Shipping policy"
- [ ] Customize theme → Footer → confirm all 4 policies + Contact are linked
- [ ] Visit each policy page and check it loads at `/policies/refund-policy`, etc.

Once these exist, your store is ad-platform compliant and your trust signals are dramatically stronger.
