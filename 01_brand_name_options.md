# Brand name options

## Why this matters first

Your current store is `charles-arbitrage-store.myshopify.com`. Two problems:

1. **The word "arbitrage"** — customers don't know that term in a friendly way. The ones who do recognize it associate it with "this is a middleman reselling AliExpress junk." It will torch your conversion rate. No serious DTC brand has "arbitrage" in its name.
2. **The "myshopify.com" subdomain** — every Shopify store starts with one, but customers see the URL at checkout and at every step. A `.com` domain is one of the highest-ROI trust upgrades you can make. ~$14/year.

You need to do TWO things:

- **Rename the store** in Shopify (Settings → General → Store details → Store name). This changes the brand displayed in the header, emails, etc.
- **Buy a matching .com** in Shopify (Settings → Domains → Buy new domain). Shopify will register and auto-connect it.

Pick one of the names below or use them as inspiration.

---

## Naming criteria you should apply

A good arbitrage/general-store brand name is:

- **Brandable, not descriptive** — "Bloom" beats "Best Home Goods Store"
- **Short** — 1–2 words, max 12 characters
- **Easy to spell** when said out loud (TikTok viewers will hear it before they see it)
- **Niche-flexible** — you'll want to swap products in/out without rebranding
- **Available as .com** — `.shop` and `.store` look cheap on arbitrage sites; .com is non-negotiable

## ⚠️ Avoid these patterns

- Anything with "arbitrage," "deals," "bargain," "discount," "wholesale" — kills perceived quality
- Anything with your full real name (e.g. "Charles Owens Apparel") — locks you into personal brand and looks small
- Generic "[Word] Store" or "[Word] Shop" — adds no info, weak
- Made-up words with random consonants (Zexbra, Quikflo) — look spammy
- Anything ending in `.shop`, `.store`, `.online`, `.co` — read as low-quality on arbitrage stores

---

## My recommendation

**Default pick: `Northhall`** if you want to play it safe and look premium.
**Wildcard: `Lookee`** if you're committing to TikTok and want a name viewers will remember.

Once you pick a name:

1. Go to https://www.namecheap.com or Shopify → Settings → Domains
2. Check `.com` availability. If taken, try `getNAME.com`, `shopNAME.com`, or `NAMEgoods.com` — but only as fallback
3. Buy it (~$14/year)
4. In Shopify: Settings → General → rename store to your new brand
5. In Shopify: Online Store → Themes → Customize → update logo text and any header references
