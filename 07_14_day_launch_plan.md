# 14-day launch plan: from "store exists" to "first sale"

Designed for: $20 budget, ~1–2 hours/day commitment, zero existing audience.

The North Star metric for this 14 days: **first sale.**
Stretch goal: **5 sales by Day 14.**

---

## How this plan is structured

- **Days 1–3 (Foundation):** Fix the store so it can actually convert when traffic shows up
- **Days 4–7 (Content + Network):** Drive first traffic via free channels
- **Days 8–14 (Iterate + Amplify):** Double down on what's working, get to first sale

---

## Day 1 — Unlock the store + rebrand

**Time: ~90 min · Cost: ~$14 (domain)**

- [ ] Settings → Online store → Preferences → **remove password protection** (your store is currently inaccessible to customers — this is THE most urgent fix)
- [ ] Pick a brand name from `01_brand_name_options.md`
- [ ] Settings → General → rename store
- [ ] Settings → Domains → buy `.com` (~$14)
- [ ] Replace announcement bar with copy from `02_store_copy.md` § 1
- [ ] Replace hero headline + subhead + button (`02_store_copy.md` § 2)
- [ ] **Replace the cartoon mountain hero image** — pull a clean lifestyle photo from Unsplash for now (search your category) or use a phone-shot product flatlay

**Why first:** A store no one can buy from won't have a first sale. The password lock is a literal sale-blocker.

---

## Day 2 — Trust pages + policies

**Time: ~60 min · Cost: $0**

- [ ] Create About page (paste copy from `02_store_copy.md` § 3)
- [ ] Create Contact page (paste copy from `02_store_copy.md` § 4)
- [ ] Settings → Policies → Generate Privacy + Terms from Shopify's templates
- [ ] Paste Refund and Shipping policies from `03_policies.md`
- [ ] Footer → make sure all 4 policies + Contact + About are linked
- [ ] Test: visit `/policies/refund-policy`, `/pages/about`, `/pages/contact` — all must load

**Why this matters:** without these, Meta and TikTok will reject your ads later. Customers also bounce when they don't see a footer with policies — it's a "real store" signal.

---

## Day 3 — Product page rewrite + photos

**Time: ~2 hours · Cost: $0**

- [ ] For each product, rewrite the description using framework in `04_product_descriptions.md`
- [ ] Replace any default placeholder t-shirt images with real product images (4–5 per product minimum). Pull from supplier or photograph yourself.
- [ ] Add the guarantee block to every product page
- [ ] Set prices: aim for **3–4× your cost** (after Shopify fees, shipping, and ad spend you keep ~30–40% as gross profit)
- [ ] Test the full purchase flow: add to cart → checkout → use Shopify's "Bogus" test gateway to make sure checkout works

**By end of Day 3, your store should be presentable to a stranger.** This is the bar.

---

## Day 4 — Email + automation setup

**Time: ~45 min · Cost: $0**

- [ ] Activate abandoned cart automation (paste copy from `05_email_automations.md`)
- [ ] Build welcome series automation (3 emails)
- [ ] Build post-purchase automation (1 email at +7 days)
- [ ] Install free reviews app (Loox or Judge.me)
- [ ] Install signup popup (free Shopify Forms app — 10% off for email)
- [ ] Install Meta Pixel (Facebook & Instagram sales channel)
- [ ] Install TikTok Pixel (TikTok sales channel)

**Pixels especially: install today even though you're not running ads yet.** They start collecting data immediately. By the time you have $200 to spend in 2 months, you'll have a warm retargeting audience already built.

---

## Day 5 — TikTok account + first 6 videos

**Time: ~90 min · Cost: $0**

- [ ] Create TikTok account, set up bio + profile pic per `06_tiktok_content_pack.md`
- [ ] Pick your top 2 products
- [ ] Film 6 videos (3 hooks per product) using the hook list
- [ ] Post 3 today, schedule 3 for tomorrow morning
- [ ] Cross-post to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts (free additional reach)

---

## Day 6 — Network launch

**Time: ~30 min · Cost: $0**

- [ ] Post the store on your personal Instagram story — keep it casual: "Hey, I started a small online store — would mean a lot if you took a look. [link]"
- [ ] Drop in 2–3 group chats (close friends only — pick the ones who'll actually click)
- [ ] Post in any Berkeley Discord/group chats you're in
- [ ] If you have a personal Twitter/X — one tweet
- [ ] Film TikTok video #4–6, post first one, schedule rest

**Reality:** Your first sale will most likely come from someone in your network in the first 7 days. That's normal and good. You can screenshot it for a "first sale!" TikTok which is itself viral content.

---

## Day 7 — Audit + adjust

**Time: ~45 min · Cost: $0**

- [ ] Pull the week's TikTok analytics. Which video got the most views? What was the hook?
- [ ] Check Shopify analytics: how many visitors? What's the bounce rate? Cart abandonment?
- [ ] If 50+ visitors and 0 add-to-carts: your product page is the problem (rewrite)
- [ ] If add-to-carts but no checkouts: your checkout is the problem (test it; adjust shipping costs)
- [ ] Film 6 more TikTok videos. Lean into whatever hook style worked.

---

## Days 8–14 — Compound

Same loop, but optimized:

- [ ] **Daily:** Film and post 4–5 TikToks. Cross-post to Reels + Shorts.
- [ ] **Daily:** Spend 5 min replying to comments on your videos (algorithm boost)
- [ ] **Daily:** Check Shopify orders. If you have a sale: scream, screenshot it for content, fulfill within 24 hours.
- [ ] **Day 10:** Add a second round of products if first ones are getting attention but not converting. Variety helps for arbitrage stores.
- [ ] **Day 12:** Email any past customers (if you have any) asking for a review. Offer 15% off next purchase as thank-you.
- [ ] **Day 14:** Full review. What worked? What didn't? What's the plan for the next 14 days?

---

## What "success" looks like at Day 14

| Metric | Floor | Target | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store visitors | 200 | 1,000 | 5,000 |
| TikTok videos posted | 30 | 50 | 70 |
| Single best TikTok view count | 500 | 5,000 | 50,000 |
| Email subscribers | 10 | 50 | 200 |
| Sales | 0–1 | 3–5 | 10+ |

**If you hit "Floor" across the board, you're on track.** First sale often comes Day 5–10. Don't panic if Day 7 has nothing — the algorithm is testing you.

**If you hit "Target," start thinking about paid ads.** You've validated the funnel. $20/day on TikTok Spark Ads (boosting your best organic video) is the next move.

---

## What to NOT do in these 14 days

- ❌ **Don't run paid ads yet.** $20 is not enough to learn anything from ads. You'll lose all of it and conclude wrongly that ads don't work.
- ❌ **Don't add 50 more products.** Better to convert on 5 products than be ignored across 50.
- ❌ **Don't redesign the store every other day.** Ship at "good enough" by Day 3 and stop touching it.
- ❌ **Don't read about Shopify all day.** You'll fall into the "course rabbit hole" — endless YouTube videos that feel like progress but aren't. Reading this once is enough.
- ❌ **Don't quit on Day 5 because nothing happened.** This is the single biggest failure mode. The first sale almost always comes between days 7–14. The only people who fail are the ones who stop posting.
