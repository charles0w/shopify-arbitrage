# Email automations — copy/paste pack

Email is the cheapest sales channel you have. For a small store, **abandoned cart recovery alone can be 15–25% of revenue.** Set this up before you spend a dollar on ads.

> **Tools:** Use **Shopify Email** (free, comes with Shopify, fine for starting out) or **Klaviyo** (free up to 250 contacts — better automation, recommended once you cross 50+ subscribers).
>
> Where to set up in Shopify Admin: Marketing → Automations → "Create automation"

---

## Automation #1 — Welcome series (3 emails)

**Trigger:** Customer subscribes to email list (via popup or footer signup)
**Goal:** Convert the email signup into a first sale

### Email 1 — sent immediately

**Subject:** Welcome — here's your code 🎁
**Preview text:** Use WELCOME10 for 10% off your first order

```
Hey,

Thanks for joining [BRAND]. You're in.

As promised, here's 10% off your first order:

  Code: WELCOME10
  (Auto-applies at checkout — or just type it in)

I started [BRAND] because I was tired of digging through endless
junk on big marketplaces to find products actually worth buying.
Everything in the shop has passed three tests:
  1. It solves a real problem or feels good to use
  2. It's priced fairly
  3. I'd use it myself

Here are three I'd start with:

[ Product 1 — link ]
[ Product 2 — link ]
[ Product 3 — link ]

Reply to this email anytime — it goes straight to my inbox.

— Charles
[BRAND]

[ Shop bestsellers → ]
```

### Email 2 — sent 2 days later (if no purchase)

**Subject:** What people are reaching for
**Preview text:** The 3 products everyone's been ordering this week

```
Hey,

A few people asked what's been most popular this week, so:

🥇 [Bestseller 1] — [one-line benefit]
🥈 [Bestseller 2] — [one-line benefit]
🥉 [Bestseller 3] — [one-line benefit]

Your 10% code (WELCOME10) is still good for a few more days.

[ Shop now → ]

— Charles
```

### Email 3 — sent 5 days later (if no purchase)

**Subject:** Code expires tonight
**Preview text:** Last call on your welcome 10% off

```
Heads up — your WELCOME10 code expires at midnight tonight.

If you've been on the fence, this is the moment.

[ Use my code → ]

If now's not the right time, no worries — I'll keep you posted on
new arrivals and won't bombard your inbox.

— Charles
```

## Automation #2 — Abandoned cart series (2 emails)

**Trigger:** Customer adds item to cart, starts checkout, doesn't complete within 1 hour
**Goal:** Recover the sale

> Shopify has this built in — **Marketing → Automations → "Abandoned checkout."** Activate it and replace the default copy with the below.

### Email 1 — sent 1 hour after abandonment

**Subject:** Forgot something?
**Preview text:** Your cart is still here — finishing up takes 30 seconds

```
Hey,

Looks like something caught your eye but you didn't make it through
checkout. No pressure — your cart's saved and ready when you are.

[ Cart preview — auto-populated by Shopify ]

[ Complete my order → ]

If you ran into any issues at checkout (payment, shipping, anything),
just reply to this email and I'll help personally.

— Charles
[BRAND]
```

### Email 2 — sent 24 hours after abandonment (if still no purchase)

**Subject:** Still thinking it over? Here's 10% off
**Preview text:** Code COMEBACK10 — good for 48 hours

```
Hey,

Your cart is still here, and I want to make this easier:

  Code: COMEBACK10
  10% off your order — good for 48 hours

[ Apply code & checkout → ]

A few things worth knowing:
  • Free shipping on orders over $50
  • 30-day returns, no questions
  • Real human support — me, replying to your emails

— Charles
```

> ⚠️ Don't go more aggressive than this with discounts on first purchase. Stacking discounts trains customers to wait for them.

## Automation #3 — Post-purchase (1 email)

**Trigger:** 7 days after first order is delivered
**Goal:** Build trust, encourage review, set up second purchase

**Subject:** How's it going with your order?
**Preview text:** Quick check-in from [BRAND]

```
Hey,

Saw your [ORDER ITEM] should've landed about a week ago. Quick
check-in:

How's it working out for you?

If you love it, would you mind sharing a quick review? It helps
other shoppers more than you'd think:

[ Leave a review → ]

If anything's not right, hit reply — I'll fix it.

And if you want a few more finds I think you'd like:

[ Shop new arrivals → ]

— Charles
```

> Reviews are a force multiplier on a small store. Even 5 reviews per product massively lifts conversion. Reviews app: **Loox** (free tier) or **Judge.me** (free).

---

## Setup checklist

In Shopify Admin → Marketing → Automations:

- [ ] Activate the built-in "Abandoned checkout" automation, paste in Email #1 and add Email #2 as a follow-up
- [ ] Create new automation "Welcome series" with the 3 emails above (trigger: customer subscribes)
- [ ] Create new automation "Post-purchase check-in" (trigger: 7 days after delivery)
- [ ] In Settings → Notifications: customize the order confirmation email with your brand voice (most stores skip this — it's a free chance to set tone)
- [ ] Install a free reviews app (Loox or Judge.me) so the post-purchase email has somewhere to send reviewers

This entire pack should take ~30 minutes to set up and will run on autopilot forever after.
