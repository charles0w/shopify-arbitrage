## What I found in your store

I audited the screenshot you sent. **Three critical issues, in order of urgency:**

### 1. 🚨 Your store is password-protected — no customer can buy from it

The bottom banner of your screenshot reads "Password protected." That means your store is **not actually live to customers.** "Live products" in Shopify admin ≠ live store. You are currently invisible to the internet.

**Fix (60 seconds):** Settings → Online Store → Preferences → "Disable password" → Save.

This is your first action when you finish reading.

### 2. 🚨 The brand name "charles-arbitrage-store" is killing trust

"Arbitrage" is industry insider language that signals "dropshipper reselling AliExpress goods" to anyone who knows the term — and reads as weird and unprofessional to anyone who doesn't. No real DTC brand has it in the name. You will not get sales with this brand name regardless of what else you do.

**Fix:** Pick a brand name from `01_brand_name_options.md`. My top pick: **Northhall**. Then buy the matching `.com` (~$14, your single best use of the $20 budget).

### 3. ⚠️ The store is using Shopify default placeholder graphics

The mountain illustration in your hero and the t-shirt silhouettes in your product grid are **default Shopify placeholders.** Customers recognize these instantly and bounce. They mean either "this store hasn't done its homework" or "this store is fake." Neither sells.

**Fix:** Day 3 of the 14-day plan walks through replacing all of them. Free options included.
